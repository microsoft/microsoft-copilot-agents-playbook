🍝 Authentic Pasta al Pomodoro (Spaghetti al Pomodoro)

One of the great classics of Italian cuisine, spaghetti al pomodoro is deceptively simple: excellent tomatoes, good olive oil, fresh basil, and pasta cooked al dente make all the difference. 🇮🇹

Ingredients (4 servings)

  • 360 g spaghetti
  • 800 g canned peeled tomatoes (pomodori pelati)
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Fresh basil
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Source recipe: Cucchiaio d’Argento. 1

Method

  1. In a saucepan, gently heat extra virgin olive oil with the garlic clove (halved and with the germ removed). Add the peeled tomatoes, season with salt, cover, and cook over low heat for about 30 minutes. 1
  2. Remove the garlic and pass the tomatoes through a food mill (passaverdure) to obtain a smooth, pulpy sauce. 1
  3. Return the sauce to the pan and cook uncovered for another 10 minutes so it thickens slightly. 1
  4. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in plenty of salted water until al dente. Drain and transfer directly to the sauce. Toss briefly to coat the pasta evenly. 1
  5. Serve immediately with fresh basil and a little freshly ground black pepper. 1

Chef’s Tip 👨‍🍳

For truly buonissimo results, use high-quality Italian peeled tomatoes and avoid overcooking the basil. Add it at the end to preserve its fresh aroma. Also, reserve a small ladle of pasta water in case the sauce needs loosening while you toss the pasta.

Prompt 2: How do I make a classic tiramisù from scratch?

Ah, il tiramisù classico 🇮🇹🍮! One of the most beloved desserts in Italian tradition. Below is a traditional version sourced from Il Cucchiaio d'Argento.

Ingredients (serves 6)

  • 400 g mascarpone
  • 200 g savoiardi (ladyfingers)
  • 150 g icing sugar
  • 200 g dark chocolate, grated
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 2 egg whites
  • 3 small cups of strong espresso coffee, cooled
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder for dusting

Source recipe: 1

Method

  1. Prepare the cream
  2. Separate the egg yolks and whites.
  3. Whisk the egg whites until stiff peaks form. 1
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg yolks with the icing sugar until pale and fluffy. 1
  5. Gently fold in the mascarpone. 1

  6. Carefully fold in the whipped egg whites to keep the mixture light and airy. 1

  7. Assemble the tiramisù

  8. Arrange a layer of savoiardi in a deep serving dish.
  9. Lightly brush or quickly dip them in the cooled coffee. Do not soak them too much. 1
  10. Spread a layer of mascarpone cream over the biscuits.
  11. Sprinkle with grated dark chocolate. 1

  12. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of cream. 1

  13. Finish and chill

  14. Dust generously with unsweetened cocoa powder.
  15. Refrigerate for about 3 hours before serving. 1

Chef's Tip 👨‍🍳

The secret to a buonissimo tiramisù is restraint when dipping the savoiardi. A quick dip keeps them soft yet structured. Also, use room-temperature mascarpone for a smoother cream, and let the tiramisù rest overnight if possible for even better flavor. ✨

Prompt 3: What tools and ingredients do I need to make a great risotto?

Evaluators: RelevanceCoherence

Ah, risotto! 🍚 One of the great classics of Italian cuisine. The secret to a great risotto is not just the recipe, but having the right tools and quality ingredients ready in your mise en place all'italiana.

Essential Tools 👨‍🍳

1. Wide, heavy-bottomed pan or sauté pan

  • A wide surface helps the rice cook evenly.
  • Heavy-bottomed cookware distributes heat gently and prevents scorching.

2. Saucepan for the stock

  • Keep the broth hot throughout cooking.
  • Adding cold stock can disrupt the cooking process.

3. Wooden spoon or silicone spatula

  • Gentle stirring helps release starch without breaking the grains.

4. Ladle

  • For adding stock gradually, one scoop at a time.

5. Sharp knife and cutting board

  • For shallots, onions, mushrooms, herbs, or other ingredients.

Nice-to-have tools

  • Microplane or fine grater for Parmigiano Reggiano 🧀
  • Kitchen scale for precise measurements
  • Small tasting spoon (you'll use it often!)

Essential Ingredients 🇮🇹

1. Risotto rice

Use one of these traditional Italian varieties: - Carnaroli (many chefs' favorite; excellent texture) - Vialone Nano (very creamy) - Arborio (widely available and beginner-friendly)

2. Quality stock (brodo)

  • Chicken stock for many classic risotti.
  • Vegetable stock for vegetarian versions.
  • Homemade is ideal, but a well-made stock is the foundation of flavor.

3. Shallot or onion

  • Finely chopped and gently softened.
  • Shallot provides a more delicate flavor.

4. Dry white wine

  • Added after toasting the rice (tostatura).
  • Look for a crisp, dry style.

5. Butter

  • Used at the end for mantecatura, the final emulsifying step that creates the silky texture.

6. Parmigiano Reggiano

  • Freshly grated.
  • Adds richness and umami.

7. Salt and pepper

  • Season carefully since stock and cheese may already contain salt.

The Classic Risotto Formula

For 4 people:

  • 320 g risotto rice
  • 1 to 1.5 L hot stock
  • 1 shallot
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 40 g butter (plus extra for finishing)
  • 60 to 80 g Parmigiano Reggiano
  • Salt and pepper

Professional Chef Tips ✨

Toast the rice (tostatura)

Cook the rice in the pan for 1 to 2 minutes before adding wine. This helps the grains retain their structure.

Keep the stock hot

A simmering stock integrates more smoothly and keeps cooking consistent.

Stir, but don't overdo it

Gentle, frequent stirring is enough. Constant aggressive stirring can make the texture gummy.

Finish off the heat

The famous mantecatura is done with butter and Parmigiano after removing the pan from the heat. This creates the creamy consistency Italians call "all'onda" ("like a wave"), where the risotto gently flows when the pan is shaken.

Use good cheese

Freshly grated Parmigiano Reggiano makes a noticeable difference. Buonissimo! 🇮🇹

If you'd like, I can also share an authentic risotto recipe from one of the trusted Italian sources such as GialloZafferano, Il Cucchiaio d'Argento, or Fatto in Casa da Benedetta. 🍚✨

Certo! 😊 Per un tiramisù classico, puoi sostituire il mascarpone con il cream cheese, ma il risultato sarà un po' diverso dall'originale italiano.

Sostituzione mascarpone → cream cheese

Il mascarpone è più ricco, dolce e morbido. Il cream cheese è più compatto e leggermente acidulo.

Per ottenere una crema più equilibrata, ti consiglio:

  • 100% cream cheese: funziona, ma il gusto sarà più simile a una cheesecake.
  • Meglio ancora: 75% cream cheese + 25% panna fresca montata per avvicinarti maggiormente alla consistenza del mascarpone. 👨‍🍳

Adattamento per 12 persone

La ricetta classica del Cucchiaio d'Argento è per 6 persone e utilizza: 400 g mascarpone, 200 g savoiardi, 4 tuorli, 2 albumi, 150 g zucchero a velo e 3 tazzine di caffè. 1

Per 12 persone, raddoppia le quantità:

| Ingrediente | Per 12 persone | |------------|---------------| | Cream cheese | 800 g | | Savoiardi | 400 g | | Tuorli | 8 | | Albumi | 4 | | Zucchero a velo | 300 g | | Caffè | 6 tazzine | | Cacao amaro | q.b. |

Procedimento adattato

  1. Prepara il caffè e lascialo raffreddare.
  2. Monta gli albumi a neve ferma.
  3. In una ciotola capiente, monta i tuorli con lo zucchero fino a ottenere un composto chiaro e spumoso.
  4. Aggiungi il cream cheese poco alla volta fino a ottenere una crema liscia.
  5. Incorpora delicatamente gli albumi montati, mescolando dal basso verso l'alto (delicatamente, come direbbe una nonna italiana! 🇮🇹).
  6. Disponi un primo strato di savoiardi leggermente inzuppati nel caffè.
  7. Copri con metà della crema.
  8. Ripeti con un secondo strato di savoiardi e il resto della crema.
  9. Lascia riposare in frigorifero per almeno 3 ore.
  10. Spolvera con cacao amaro appena prima di servire.

Consiglio dello chef 🍮

Poiché la tua ricetta usa uova crude, utilizza uova molto fresche oppure valuta una versione con uova pastorizzate, particolarmente consigliata quando prepari il dolce per un gruppo numeroso.

Serves: 4
Difficulty: Easy
Preparation: 10 min
Cooking: 25 min 1

Ingredients

  • 320 g Carnaroli rice

  • 1.5 liters meat broth
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 sachet saffron (or saffron threads infused in warm broth) 1

Method

  1. Prepare the brodo di carne (meat broth). If using saffron threads, steep them in a cup of warm broth for at least 1 hour to develop color and aroma. 1
  2. Remove the marrow from the bone and chop it roughly. 1
  3. In a wide casserole, melt 30 g of butter with the marrow. Add the finely chopped onion and cook gently until translucent. 1
  4. Add the rice and toast it (tostatura) until the grains look glossy. 1
  5. Begin adding hot broth, one ladle at a time, allowing each addition to be absorbed before adding the next. 1
  6. About halfway through cooking, stir in the saffron infusion and continue cooking, adding broth gradually. 1
  7. After 15 to 18 minutes, when the rice is al dente, remove from the heat. 1
  8. Mantecare (finish the risotto) with the remaining butter and grated cheese. Cover for a couple of minutes, then serve immediately. 1
